## 1. Is there any credible shared CTD signal? — **Weak, and on one construct only**

**Binary labels: no.** Of the 22 live features, **zero** reach BH-FDR q ≤ 0.10
in both corpora. Sign agreement is 12/22, i.e. exactly chance (binomial
p = 0.83). The decisive number is the search-aware one: PDCH's single
best-looking feature has |r| = 0.255, and the subject-grouped permutation null
for *the maximum* |r| across 22 features puts that at **p = 0.34** with a 95th
percentile of 0.345. PDCH's best binary association is indistinguishable from
what searching 22 correlated features over 62 sessions produces by itself.
DAIC-WOZ's own max is only marginally better (|r| = 0.250, p = 0.030).

**Continuous severity: weakly, once.** `res_h` (response latency) is the only
feature that behaves the same way on both corpora against severity:

| | DAIC-WOZ `PHQ8_Score` (train+dev, n=135) | PDCH `HAMD17_total` (n=62) |
|---|---|---|
| Spearman ρ | **+0.245** | **+0.267** |
| permutation p | 0.004 | 0.086 |
| BH-q | **0.051** (passes) | 0.431 (fails) |

Same sign, near-identical magnitude, different instruments and raters. It fails
the pre-declared confirmation rule because PDCH does not clear FDR — but it
fails it for a reason (n = 62) rather than by pointing the other way. This is
the one result in the audit that is shaped like shared signal.

**It does not survive as a detector.** E3 ran `res_h` alone, as pre-declared:

| | DAIC dev | DAIC **test** | DAIC pooled CV | PDCH subject-grouped CV |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| macro-F1 | 0.581 | 0.779 | 0.595 ± 0.006 | 0.575 ± 0.012 |
| ROC AUC | — | 0.829 | 0.626 ± 0.009 | 0.591 ± 0.032 |

Pre-declared criterion (PDCH ≥ 0.60 macro-F1 **and** ≥ 0.60 AUC; DAIC test
≥ 0.60 macro-F1): **FAIL — DAIC-WOZ only.**

**A correction the paper needs.** That DAIC test column is `PF-05`'s
headline — `res_h` at test macro-F1 0.779 / AUC 0.829, higher than the full
24-D model. The same feature, same pipeline, scores **0.581 on dev** and
**0.595 ± 0.006 under pooled cross-validation**. The 0.829 is an artifact of
one 45-session split, not a property of `res_h`. `PF-05` should be closed as
*not reproducible under resampling*, and the paper must not build on it. This
is the cleanest single answer to `SLT-02` we have.

### The audit's four buckets

- **(A) Shared signal:** `res_h` ↔ continuous severity, positive on both
  corpora, ρ ≈ 0.25. Real but too small to carry a detector at either n.
- **(B) Corpus-specific:** `ask_d` is the only feature clearing FDR on
  DAIC-WOZ (r = −0.250, q = 0.066) — and its PDCH sign is **reversed**
  (r = +0.231). Also PDCH-only: a response-timing association with
  clinician-rated psychomotor items (§below).
- **(C) Nulls with an identified cause:** `ask_bt`/`res_bt` (structurally
  unmeasurable under mono webrtcvad — not weak, *absent*); the whole ask-side
  tier (interviewer-regime-specific, see the `ask_d` reversal); 12 of the 22
  features (algebraically redundant — see §3).
- **(D) Open, needs `MC-04`/`MC-09`:** whether the residual PDCH gap beyond
  sample size is language, instrument, or interviewer regime. The audit can
  separate power from corpus but cannot separate those three.

## 2. Is the PDCH null confounds or construct failure? — **Neither; it is mostly power, with a construct component**

The brief offered two explanations. The audit rejects the first, partially
supports the second, and identifies a third that dominates both.

**Confounds: rejected as the primary cause.** PDCH interviews are 1.97× longer
with 2.14× the turns, so this was the leading suspect. It does not survive
testing:

- **Matching** positives to negatives on session span and turn count (optimal
  1:1 assignment, 27 pairs, span SMD −0.231 → **0.003**) leaves the null
  *exactly* where it was: `all24` macro-F1 **0.506 ± 0.049**, AUC 0.498;
  `no_ask` 0.508 ± 0.058, AUC 0.498. Criterion for "softens" was 0.60/0.60.
- **Residualizing** the six absolute-time features on log span and log turn
  count, fitted within training folds, also changes nothing on PDCH
  (0.519 → 0.492 for `all24`; AUC 0.474 → 0.479).

Residual imbalance in turn count after matching is SMD −0.313, so the check is
not perfect — but the residualization arm covers exactly that remainder and is
equally flat. A null that survives both is not a length artifact.

**Power: this is the dominant term, and it was not previously measured.**
E1c subsampled DAIC-WOZ 100 times to PDCH's exact cohort shape (n = 62 at 43.5%
positive) and ran the identical subject-grouped nested CV:

| | DAIC at n=62 | PDCH observed | PDCH's percentile in that null |
|---|---|---|---|
| macro-F1 | 0.605 ± 0.063 (5th pct 0.502) | 0.520 | **10th** (z = −1.35) |
| balanced acc. | 0.607 ± 0.063 | 0.522 | 10th (z = −1.35) |
| ROC AUC | 0.636 ± 0.075 (min 0.480) | 0.476 | **0th** (z = −2.14) |

Read carefully, this splits the null in two. On the *classification* metrics
PDCH is low but **inside** the range a corpus with known signal produces at this
sample size — a tenth of DAIC subsamples do just as badly. So "PDCH is at
chance" cannot be asserted from macro-F1 at n = 62; the experiment mostly lacks
the resolution to tell. On **AUC**, PDCH falls below every one of the 100
subsamples, so there is a genuine residual corpus difference that power alone
does not explain.

**Construct: the remainder, and it is specific.** Three identified mechanisms:

1. **The ask side is interviewer-regime-specific.** `ask_d` is the only
   FDR-surviving feature on DAIC-WOZ and it *reverses sign* on PDCH
   (−0.250 vs +0.231). On DAIC-WOZ a wizard-driven agent asks longer questions
   of less-depressed participants; a human clinician does the opposite. This is
   `multi-corpus-plan.md` §3's predicted confound appearing as data, and it
   means the ask tier cannot be pooled or compared across interviewer regimes
   at all.
2. **The ask side is also where DAIC's length-dependence lives.** E1b's DAIC
   control: residualizing drops `all24` from macro-F1 0.649 → 0.609 and AUC
   0.700 → 0.658, while `no_ask` is untouched (0.601 → 0.603, AUC
   0.660 → 0.663). So roughly a fifth of the deployed detector's above-chance
   AUC is session-length information carried by ask-side features. That must be
   disclosed; a reviewer who runs this check will find it.
3. **Two features are not measurable, not weak.** `ask_bt`/`res_bt` are exactly
   constant on PDCH; mono webrtcvad cannot represent overlap.

**Verdict.** The PDCH null is **not** a confound artifact. It is, in order of
size: (i) an under-powered binary target at n = 62 — DAIC would look only
marginally above chance there too; (ii) a real but modest corpus difference
visible in AUC; (iii) an ask-side tier that is regime-specific by construction
and should never have been expected to transfer.

## 3. What should the ICASSP story be, given this audit alone?

**Not "CTD transfers."** Also **not** the flat "CTD is corpus-specific" claim
that `action-items.md` provisionally adopted on 2026-09-23 — the power
calibration shows PDCH at n = 62 cannot distinguish "no signal" from
"DAIC-sized signal" on classification metrics, so that claim overstates what
62 sessions can support.

The defensible paper is **an audited negative with a mechanism, plus two
methodological corrections that are novel in their own right**:

1. **The negative, correctly bounded.** Dyadic CTD does not transfer from
   English wizard-led screening to Mandarin clinician-led inpatient interviews.
   Reported *with* its power calibration, so the claim is "not detectable at
   n = 62, and below the DAIC-at-n=62 AUC range," not "absent."
2. **The mechanism.** The ask-side tier is interviewer-regime-specific — the
   `ask_d` sign reversal is direct evidence — and it simultaneously carries
   session-length information (E1b). The relational/cross-speaker reading that
   `SLT-05` established on DAIC-WOZ survives; the interviewer-side reading does
   not.
3. **`res_h` is real but small, and the published test number is not.** The
   response-latency ↔ severity association replicates in sign and magnitude
   across two instruments (ρ ≈ 0.25 on both), which is the strongest support the
   psychomotor-retardation story has ever had — *and* the 0.829 test AUC that
   made it look decisive is an n = 45 artifact (dev 0.581, pooled CV 0.595).
   Publishing both halves is a direct, self-generated answer to `SLT-02`.
4. **The descriptor is not 24-D.** Turn duration is exactly speech + silence,
   so `*_ud`, `*_sd`, `*_du`, `*_su`, `*_ds`, `*_us` are six monotone
   transforms of one number per speaker — verified to 4.4e-16 on both corpora.
   Twelve of the 22 live features carry two underlying quantities; the centred
   22-feature matrix has **rank 13 on both corpora**. `PF-02` found rank 15 of
   24 on DAIC-WOZ pairwise; this is the algebraic reason. A "compact 24-D
   descriptor" that is really 13-D is worth stating ourselves.

This is a `SLT-01` + `SLT-02` + `SLT-03` paper: generalization tested and
reported honestly, the central claim's statistical fragility diagnosed from the
inside, and a novelty hook (interviewer-regime dependence of dyadic timing)
that no reviewer proposed.

**Flagged lead, not a claim.** On PDCH only, response-internal timing tracks
*clinician-rated* psychomotor items where it does not track the HAMD-17 total
or the binary label: response silence fraction vs. HAMD item 8 (retardation)
ρ = −0.331, permutation p = 0.008, BH-q = 0.062; `res_h` vs. item 9 (agitation)
ρ = +0.296, q = 0.066. DAIC's self-reported `PHQ8_Moving` shows nothing
(max |ρ| = 0.195, p = 0.19) — consistent with self-report being a poor index of
observable psychomotor change. **But** the search-aware statistic is only
borderline (max-|ρ| permutation p = 0.072 for item 8, 0.102 for item 9), item 8
is non-zero in just 18 of 62 sessions, and items 8 and 9 correlate positively
with each other (ρ = 0.338) rather than behaving as opposite poles — item 9
correlates 0.615 with the total, so part of that column is severity, not
agitation. Report as a lead to pre-register for a future cohort. Do **not**
report it as a finding.

## 4. `MC-04` vs `MC-09` — recommend **`MC-04` (E-DAIC-Extended) as the next P0**

**Recommendation: `MC-04`.** The audit leaves exactly two live explanations for
the residual PDCH gap — sample size, and a corpus difference that is some
mixture of language, instrument and interviewer regime. `MC-04` is the only
available experiment that holds three of those four constant: E-DAIC-Extended
(sessions 600–718) is the same language, the same instrument (PHQ-8), the same
label rule and the same annotation pipeline as DAIC-WOZ, and changes **only**
the interviewer regime, from wizard-driven to fully autonomous. That is a
direct test of the one concrete mechanism this audit produced — the `ask_d`
sign reversal — and it is the only way to learn whether the ask tier's
regime-dependence is the whole story or just part of it. It also runs at n = 86
rather than 62, which E1c shows is where this design starts to resolve anything
at all, and it does so on subjects disjoint from DAIC-WOZ (`MC-11`). `MC-09`, by
contrast, would add a semantic/acoustic branch to a corpus whose timing channel
is both at chance and under-powered: it cannot separate power from construct,
it produces a fusion number with no usable CTD contribution to fuse, and it
answers a completeness question rather than the generalization question that
rejected the paper. Keep `MC-09` for the PDCH write-up's missing-modality
disclosure, and schedule it after `MC-04`. The honest cost note: `MC-04`
depends on `MC-03` (E-DAIC ships no speaker column), and diarization is the real
expense — but the autonomous TTS voice makes it the most tractable diarization
target in the corpus set, and without it the interviewer axis in
`multi-corpus-plan.md` §3 stays a two-point line.
